# RDC AI — Naming Notes

The working name is **RDC AI**. No final product name has been selected.

## What the name should communicate

The product is:

- local-first;
- able to continue one conversation across Windows, iPhone, local AI, and cloud AI;
- intentionally user-controlled when moving from local to cloud;
- part of the Royal Digital Clarity ecosystem without needing to sound like an internal engineering tool;
- broader than a single model or provider.

Avoid names that lock the product to:

- Foundry;
- Azure;
- a specific model vendor;
- only local AI;
- only cloud AI.

## Candidate directions

### Continuity / movement

- **RDC Continuum** — emphasizes one conversation across devices and providers.
- **RDC Relay** — emphasizes handing a conversation between local device and cloud.
- **RDC Thread** — the conversation remains the durable thread while models change.
- **RDC Flow** — simple, approachable, and consistent with moving between inference locations.
- **RDC Bridge** — focuses on the connection between local and cloud environments.

### Local-first / personal workspace

- **RDC Haven** — suggests a private/local place with optional cloud reach.
- **RDC Anchor** — local conversation is the stable anchor while providers can change.
- **RDC Homebase** — strongly communicates local-first, though less polished as a product brand.
- **RDC Pocket** — good mobile/local connotation, but may understate the Windows/cloud experience.

### Intelligence / navigation

- **RDC Compass** — helps navigate work while preserving user control.
- **RDC Beacon** — local intelligence with the ability to reach outward.
- **RDC Nexus** — emphasizes connected devices/providers, though the word is heavily used in technology.
- **RDC Orbit** — conversations move among devices/providers while staying around one user/workspace.

## Strongest current candidates

These are not decisions, just the current shortlist:

1. **RDC Continuum**
2. **RDC Relay**
3. **RDC Thread**
4. **RDC Compass**
5. **RDC Anchor**

Before selecting a public product name, perform a current trademark/product/domain conflict check.
